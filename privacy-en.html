RopeDelivery Privacy Policy
Developer/brand: Diverse.Studio
App: RopeDelivery (Android)
Package: com.p0lab.threaddelivery
Effective: October 12, 2026
Support and privacy contact: diversestudio.support@gmail.com

1. Scope
RopeDelivery is a physics puzzle in which players swipe to cut ropes and deliver a gift capsule. The current closed-test version does not include accounts, developer servers, cloud saves, real advertising, billing, analytics or remote crash-reporting SDKs. It can be played offline. Its intended audience is teenagers aged 13 and older and adults. This audience description does not replace regional content ratings.

2. Local game information
The app stores the selected stage, unlock and delivery records, the best stamp count from one successful attempt, in-game gold, unlocked hints, owned and equipped skins, and result records used to prevent duplicate rewards in Android app-private local storage. These records restore gameplay and purchase state. Purchases use gold earned inside the game and are not real-money transactions.
The app does not transmit this information to developer servers or advertising or analytics providers. This closed-test build disables Android automatic app backup and does not offer cloud synchronization. The development reward simulator is not exposed in the Play test build.

3. Permissions and information not requested
Gameplay does not require a name, email address, birth date, location, contacts, advertising identifier, photos, microphone recordings or payment information to be provided to the developer. The app does not request camera, microphone, location or contacts permissions. A policy web link opens the public page in the default browser without attaching game progress.

4. Retention, deletion and security
Local records remain until changed in gameplay, cleared through Android app-storage settings or removed with the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling can erase progress. The developer cannot remotely view, delete or restore device-only records. The app uses Android app-private storage access controls; it does not claim additional file encryption. There is no developer-server account to delete.

5. Support messages
If you email support, the recipient receives your email address, message and information you choose to attach. These are used to respond and resolve the issue. Send only necessary material, not passwords, signing keys, identity documents or card details. After resolving the purpose, the operator reviews received and sent emails, attachments and separate copies to remove unnecessary personal information, subject to applicable retention obligations. Automated deletion or immediate total erasure is not promised. Requests to access, correct or delete support information actually held can be sent to diversestudio.support@gmail.com.

6. Providers and this public webpage
Support uses Gmail; Google processes email transmission and storage under its own policy. Google Play and device platforms may independently process installation, review and other platform information. This is separate from the game's local saves.
This static policy site has no first-party advertisements, analytics or tracking scripts, login, forms or first-party cookies. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process IP-address access logs for security. Hosting logs are not game-progress collection by the developer. Google and GitHub may process information outside your country under their policies and applicable law.

7. Changes
Statements about no advertising or analytics describe this closed-test build. If SDKs or information handling change, the policy and effective date will be updated for the new build with necessary notices or consent. Policies for other Diverse.Studio games do not describe RopeDelivery.
